Subject: [PATCH] Editor: fix no-scroll and dotted focus box with a stale
 cached styles.css

styles.css was loaded without a version tag, so browsers kept the old
copy. Without the new #editor rules the editor grew to the full note
height (366,000 px for a 1.5 MB note): the page could not scroll, the
cursor went off screen, and CodeMirror's default dotted focus outline
showed.

- editor.js now carries its own layout styles (height 100%, inner
  scrolling, no focus outline, padding), so a stale or missing
  styles.css cannot break it.
- index.php and list.php load styles.css?v=<mtime>, like the scripts.
